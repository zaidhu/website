## CARD COMPONENT RULES
Every product card should contain:
- Product Name
- Tagline
- Description
- Features
- Side Effects
- Compatibility Rating
- Tiny labels like:
  - Experimental
  - GEN-4 Certified
  - Trending
  - Student Favorite

## HUMOR RULE
Never use loud meme humor.
The company takes itself seriously.
The humor comes from subtle corporate absurdity.

GOOD:
"Minor existential instability may occur."

BAD:
"LOL YOUR BRAIN EXPLODES."

## FINAL GOAL
The website should feel like:
"A trillion-dollar biotech corporation with questionable ethics but amazing branding."
